# Workout Library

Named sessions that weekly plans reference. Keeps weekly plans short; update as new workouts are added.

---

## Running

### Easy Run (E)

Conversational pace. HR capped to 145 via watch monitor. Nose-breathing sustainable most of the way. No pace target — effort-based.

### Long Run (LR)

Continuous easy effort. Start conservative, OK to drift toward upper Z2 in the final third. Practice fueling if >75 min. Carry water on Whidbey hills.

### Tempo (T)

15–25 min at comfortably-hard / half-marathon effort. HR upper Z3 / lower Z4. Warm-up 10 min easy, cool-down 10 min easy.

### Marathon Pace (MP)

Segments at goal marathon pace (TBD). Used during Phase 3 specific block as segments inside long runs (e.g., "16 mi with last 6 at MP").

### Strides (ST)

Structured workout step, "4× stride-relax," 25s each (extended from 20s so GPS captures the build — validated 8/20/26, all 4 captured), full recovery between. Fired after the easy mileage as its own segment, never embedded. Target cadence 185+, HR ≤155, relaxed-fast not sprint. Neuromuscular, not aerobically taxing. Watch records strides as structured laps — same pipeline the tempo sessions use.

### Long-Run Gel Protocol (validated 8/22/26)

Gel at ~35 / 70 / 105 min, no caffeine, plain water ad lib alongside, no Tailwind on gel days. One variable at a time. Homemade standard: 150ml flask dosed in thirds at those marks (~33g each ≈ 40 g/hr; recipe in fitness-baseline.md), flask-in-pocket carry. Debut 8/22 = zero GI, energy high throughout. Commercial packets: first trial on a short run Fri 8/28, long-run debut 9/5; tear-open mechanics and rates >50 g/hr still to rehearse.

### Shakeout

15–25 min very easy + 4 strides. 1–2 days before race.

## Cycling

### Sunday Recovery Spin (Zwift)

30–45 min, Z1/low Z2, IF ≤0.65, ≥85 rpm. Standing post-long-run slot during the marathon build. Reference session: 8/16/26 — 105W avg, 93% Z1, TSS 13.

### Zwift Z2 Endurance

Steady Z2 (127–170W), flat or rolling route. 60–180 min. Two-bottle fueling if >90 min. Cadence target >= 85.

### Sweet Spot 2×20

2 × 20 min at 88–94% FTP (200–213W), 10 min rest between. Total ~75 min with warm-up/cool-down.

### Threshold 2×15

2 × 15 min at 95–102% FTP (216–231W), 8 min rest. Total ~75 min.

### VO2 5×3

5 × 3 min at 110–115% FTP (250–260W), 3 min rest. Total ~60 min. Reserve for early STP build, not peak weeks.

### Outdoor Long Ride (OLR)

Z2-dominant with terrain-forced Z3 bumps on climbs. **Nutrition protocol rehearsal:** target [g carbs/hr], [mg sodium/hr], note first discomfort point (contact, saddle, hands, feet, neck). Hand-position rotation every 20-30 minutes. Post-ride log should include these alongside power metrics.

### STP Simulation Weekend

Back-to-back long rides. Saturday = target block distance, Sunday = 50–75% of Saturday at easy Z2. Tests overnight recovery + day-2 fueling.

## Strength

### Lower Focus

Main lift: squat or hinge variation. Accessories: single-leg work (split squats, step-ups), core, carries. 3–4 working sets on main lift, moderate load (RPE 6–7 during peak sport phases).

### Upper Focus

Main lift: overhead press or row variation. Accessories: pull-ups/lat pull, push-up variations, carries. 3–4 working sets, RPE 6–7.

### Full Body Maintenance

1 compound per pattern (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry). Lower volume, moderate load. Used in race weeks and peak sport weeks.

- Prioritize movement quality over load
- Skip strength in any week with >6 hr cycling OR >35 mpw running without explicit plan adjustment
